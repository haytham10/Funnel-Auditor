**Rank 1 — the free diagnostic promises no gate, three times, then gates the result**
Depth: DEEP · Type: Broken promise at peak intent (results gated after being promised open)
Innocent explanation: /audit and /audit/start were almost certainly written at different times by different hands — /audit/start already carries the honest version of the line ("No email required until you want your results"), so the copy on /audit is a stale promise nobody went back to reconcile after the results-gate was added, not a bait-and-switch.
Why it matters: his /audit page, the destination of the single loudest CTA on his site (the sticky "Take the Founder's Audit" button on every page, the homepage hero, and the footer's "Start Here"), promises three separate times, verbatim, "You'll see your full result the moment you finish — your pattern named, the cost estimated, one next step. No gate, no pitch, no waiting." The diagnostic itself is gated. After the 12 honest questions about how their business fails without them, the founder hits: "Where should we send your results? We'll email you a unique link to your full report. First Name / Email Address / Show My Full Result." The gate is real and it lands exactly where the promise said it would not be. 847 founders have taken it. Every one of them was told there was no gate and then met one at the moment their intent was highest and their guard was lowest.
