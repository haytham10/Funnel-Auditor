## Findings — reasoning

**Rank 1 — six-option booking menu with no "start here"**
Depth: not explicitly labeled; reads DEEP — it sits at the single chokepoint every site CTA is routed through, at the exact moment of peak buying intent.
Type: booking-flow friction (undifferentiated appointment menu)
Innocent explanation: she added Acuity appointment types over time for different audiences (podcast recording, regular-client sessions, community chats) and never split the single public booking link from the internal ones, so all six surface to a cold prospect.
Why it matters: every "Let's Chat / Book a call" button on the site drops a first-time visitor onto a menu of six appointment types — three of them (Podcast Recording, Coaching Session for regular clients, Chat With My Community) are explicitly not for a stranger — with no default and no "start here." A ready prospect has to guess which of six doors is hers, and the wrong pick, or the hesitation itself, is where the booking is lost. This is the single funnel-wide chokepoint: every CTA on the site feeds this one page.
Loom skeleton (not sent, kept for a walkthrough offer): show the booking screen a cold visitor actually lands on (ghadabkhalifeh.as.me/schedule/3e2c7e47, the six-option "Select Appointment" menu); fix would be moving Podcast Recording, Coaching Session, and Chat With My Community onto a private/direct scheduling link in Acuity and pointing every "Let's Chat / Book a call" button at a single Discovery Call scheduler; done state is a first-time visitor clicking "Let's Chat" and seeing one clear "Book your free discovery call" screen with no guessing.
