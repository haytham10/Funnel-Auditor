## Findings — reasoning

**Rank 1 — all four core service "rates" buttons dead-end on a "Coming Soon" page**
Depth: not explicitly labeled; reads DEEP — it blocks every one of her four core service lines from a visitor with active buying intent, not a peripheral page.
Type: dead CTA path (services detail/rates page not yet live)
Innocent explanation: she's mid-refresh of her packages (the page is titled "Rates & Services" and reads "coming soon") — the detail/rates pages are temporarily down, not neglected.
Why it matters: the four core services' "VIEW DETAILS + RATES" buttons (Marketing Consultancy, Marketing Coaching, Social Media Management, Local SEO) all route to a "Coming Soon / Rates & Services" placeholder — a visitor with buying intent who wants to see what a service includes and costs hits a wall and is bounced to a call/WhatsApp instead.
Loom skeleton (not sent, kept for a walkthrough offer): show thebrandcoach.ae/work-with-kelly, the four service tiles, then clicking any "VIEW DETAILS + RATES" and landing on the /coming-soon page; fix would be pointing each service tile's button at a live rates/details page (or straight at the AED 7,500 audit page) instead of /coming-soon; done state is a buyer clicking "VIEW DETAILS + RATES" seeing the service and a price and being able to act, with no dead "coming soon" bounce.
Evidence: none promoted yet.
