**Rank 1 — Every "Join Now"/"Join Workshop" CTA dead-ends at an expired workshop**

Innocent explanation: she ran the workshop last October and the Wix Events widget auto-flips to "Tickets

Why it matters: every "Join Now" / "Join Workshop" CTA site-wide (homepage banner + sales page) routes to
the CARE Program Workshop dated 18 Oct 2025 (270 days past) showing "Tickets are not on sale" — a
motivated visitor who clicks to buy hits a closed door. Show/Fix/Done from the walk's Loom skeleton: Show
homepage top banner "Join my 2-hour workshop 'The CARE Program' on 18th October" → click "Join Now" →
lands on /event-details/the-care-program-workshop-4 showing "Tickets are not on sale" and "18 Oct 2025".
Fix: in Wix Events, schedule the next CARE Program Workshop date (or point the banner and "Join Workshop"
buttons at the live "Book Your Free Discovery Call" flow) so the CTA always lands on something bookable.
Done state: clicking "Join Now" opens a live, dated workshop with open tickets (or a discovery-call
booking) instead of a closed-ticket dead end.
