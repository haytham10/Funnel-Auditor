_Compiled 2026-07-27 from three sources: the 373-row UAE Lead CRM and 122 full funnel walks (proprietary), external demand research, and a supply-side pricing map. Figures are labelled measured / modelled / anecdote._

## Bottom line
 
The market passes two of Hormozi's four indicators. The two it fails are the two that decide whether you get paid.
 
| Indicator | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Massive pain | **PASS** | "Can't get clients" is the #1 complaint in every source found |
| Purchasing power | **FAIL** | ICF measured avg coach revenue **$49,283/yr**, 11.6 hrs/week, 12.4 clients; first year **$14,484**. Most UAE coaches sit below the AED 375,000 VAT line |
| Easy to target | **FAILING** | 3 sourcing veins independently reported "mined out" at 355 rows; a 4-worker top-up returned **5 rows against a 15–20 target** |
| Growing | **PASS** | MEA leads the world on every ICF sentiment measure: 71% expect revenue growth, 72% more clients, 62% more sessions |
 
Pay-per-result pricing is the structure that neutralises the purchasing-power failure: a coach who cannot fund a AED 6,000/month retainer can pay for a call that already happened.
 
## What UAE coaches universally lack
 
**A purchasable offer.** 89 of 246 classified disqualifications (36%) died on "no funnel / no paid offer" — the largest bucket by more than double. Critically, these survived a sourcing filter that already required a reachable link to a real purchasable offer, so the true market rate is worse.
 
The shape is not "no website":
 
| What they had instead | n | % of 89 |
|---|---|---|
| Own site, free-call / contact-form only, no price, no checkout | 32 | 36% |
| Third-party directory or marketplace listing only | 21 | 24% |
| Bare social profile, no owned site | 9 | 10% |
| Site is dead (404, DNS fail, unconfigured template) | 8 | 9% |
| Not actually a coaching business | 4 | 4% |
| WhatsApp / DM-only sales path | 3 | 3% |
| Unclassified | 12 | 13% |
 
Among leads that *passed* the funnel gate, **48 of 122 still show no visible price.** Adam Ashcroft runs nine live program pages, each with its own "Investment" section; not one states a fee.
 
**An owned audience.** Of 87 walked leads with an audience-ownership verdict, **36 (41%) have no email capture of any kind** — 19 with zero capture, 17 with only a contact or booking form.
 
- Spyros Bolano — 631K IG, 1M+ cross-platform, zero opt-in anywhere, bare domain returns 404. Walk verdict: "1M+ audience is fully rented."
- Mohsinaa Ahmad — 483K IG selling a AED 19,999 program. No sales page, no booking tool, no email capture. DM-keyword gated, keyword changes almost every post, and the bio link their posts direct followers to is empty (confirmed in raw HTML).
- Marie Hondekyn — 29K IG, $97/mo Skool, **3 paying members**.
- Poppy Owen — 24,800 IG + podcast, $67/mo Skool, 20 members = **0.08% conversion**.
- Eric Akoa — 330K IG monetised by one $29 course.
**Ownership of their own customers.** 28 disqualified leads have a marketplace as their only commercial presence. Moza Alfardh sells entirely via Skilldeer; the walk notes "the only email capture is Skilldeer's own footer newsletter, not Moza's." Same shape: Lucia on iheal.ae, Rita on The Holistic Culture, Tracy Harmoush's entire subscription business on Playbook.
 
## The finding that constrains everything
 
**Audience size and price ceiling are close to uncorrelated in this market.** Lama Malaeb charges AED 18,400 per corporate workshop off **268 followers**. Marie Graver runs an AED 8,000/month fractional CFO offer off 1,861 LinkedIn. Eric Akoa has 330,000 followers and one $29 SKU.
 
The high-ticket money in UAE coaching is **B2B, corporate and relationship-sourced, not audience-sourced.** This is either a warning that audience-monetisation offers are a trap here, or evidence that nobody has built the machine. The data cannot settle it.

## What the market visibly pays for
 
Ranked by money reliably attached, from the supply-side map:
 
1. **Appointment setting / closing.** $50–500 per booked appointment; $2,000–6,000/mo retainer typical; 1,981 open appointment-setter jobs and 1,178 closer jobs live on Upwork (27 Jul 2026). Quality tiers: basic $150–300/meeting, ICP-matched $300–500, BANT-verified with no-show replacement $400–750. **This is the only category universally priced on an outcome.**
2. **Funnel / GHL / Kajabi builds.** $150–3,000 per project. Fastest-closing category found.
3. **VA / online business manager.** UAE VA labour AED 3,000–6,000/mo; one coaching-VA role at $2,500/mo (US remote).
4. **Content.** LinkedIn ghostwriting $2,000–5,000/mo typical; short-form video retainers $750–2,000/mo; coach-specific video packages $297–497/mo; podcast production $1,500–7,000/mo.
5. **Coach-marketing agencies.** $2,000–10,000/mo — and coaches resent it out loud. Verbatim, r/lifecoaching: *"I'm so frustrated with coaches approaching me wanting to help me market, if I pay them 5000 to 10,000 dollars."*
The loudest complaint across all sources is not price, it is non-delivery: *"$15K to marketing agencies and got no results"*, *"appointment setting agencies charge $4-10k/month whether they deliver or not."*

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Footprint | 157 | 64% | 50 | 3 |
| Coach Directory | 78 | **81%** | 12 | 2 |

| Podcast | 17 | 76% | 4 | 0 |
| Event Speaker | 15 | 73% | 4 | 0 |
| ICF Directory | 6 | 83% | 1 | 0 |
 
Estimate: the discoverable pool — UAE-based, solo, active, ≥1,500 audience, purchasable offer, findable by no-login search — is roughly **400–500 businesses, of which 130–170 qualify.** 373 were found in 14 days.
 
**Where the saturation call is soft, and it matters:**
 
1. **Instagram is the best-yielding vein (50% DQ, 40% reaching outreach) and the second-least worked at 20 rows.** The "mined out" conclusion rests on Google Footprint and Coach Directory — the two worst performers.
2. **Arabic-language sourcing has never been attempted.** It appears nowhere in the journal.
3. The 1,500-audience floor alone killed 47 leads, several of whom passed the funnel gate.
4. All veins are no-login and search-discoverable. Coaches living inside IG DMs, WhatsApp groups or private communities are structurally invisible to the method.
## UAE-specific structural facts
 
- **Licensing is a real cost floor.** DET professional licence (mainland sole establishment) AED 15,000–25,000; total first year AED 20,000–40,000 mainland, AED 10,000–25,000 free zone. Freelance permit ~AED 7,500–9,520.
- **Free-zone coaches cannot serve mainland UAE clients directly** without a separate permit — a structural choice between cheap setup and local market access.
- **Payments are genuinely hard.** Shopify Payments unavailable in UAE; Stripe requires a business licence. One UAE operator reports Stripe blocking 70%+ of payments as "high risk" (single anecdote, r/SmallBusinessUAE).
- **VAT registration mandatory at AED 375,000** taxable supplies. A coach at that level earns roughly double the global ICF average, so most solo coaches sit below it.
- **The "transient expat" thesis is weakly supported and probably backwards.** Betterhomes 2025: average expat stay rose to **10.5 years, from 7.5 in 2024.** No source measures coach client turnover from relocation.
- **Ramadan is a surge, not a lull.** UAE local-brand online sales +203.7% over the Ramadan period (Khaleej Times).
## What could not be found
 
- Any UAE-based coach publicly hiring for marketing, ops, content or sales. "Coach" on UAE job boards means sports, fitness or tutoring.
- Any UAE/GCC agency selling a coach-specific done-for-you offer with published pricing. All coach-vertical vendors found are US/UK.
- Any ICF Middle East revenue, fee or client-count cut. ICF publishes MEA sentiment only; the full report is paywalled at $299.
- Any credible coaching churn benchmark, globally or regionally. The one coach-churn source found labels its own numbers a "reasoned range."
- Willingness to pay, from our own data. Two price-discovery answers ever logged, both `Refused to name`.
