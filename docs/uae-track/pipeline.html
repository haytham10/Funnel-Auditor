How a lead moves through the pipeline

One coach, sourced from the open web, walked to a verified funnel finding, handed to Haytham, and closed in AED. The machine runs every stage but two — the hook approval and the send, the deliberate human gates.

Audit the funnel

Machine

The real work, one agent per lead. Machine walk (Firecrawl-primary) → mandatory vision pass → Gate 0 confirm → opener-finder assigns the lane and the strongest verified finding, banking the rest. Email shape and deliverability both checked. Held at the draft — no email written yet.

Find the hook

Haytham

Real public evidence — a LinkedIn post, a podcast, their own About page — resolves the one line only this person would recognize. Fabrication is not allowed, and no draft gets written until this line is real.

haytham-hook-finder gates the draft
05

Draft the email

Machine

A Gmail draft in Haytham's voice, built on the verified finding and the approved hook. Signed off, no operator jargon, no em-dashes. A draft is never a send.

haytham-email-draft produces Draft Ready
06

Send

Haytham

Haytham reviews and sends by hand, or schedules. The system ends at the draft — sending is human, always, and never past the daily inbox ceiling.

manual · reconciled by uae-tick produces Outreach Sent

Offer & close

Machine

The priced offer — 735 AED Track A or 2,575 AED Track B, the price quoted natively, never discounted. Gated on a verbatim discovery answer: no answer, no money email.

haytham-email-draft · crm-gate offer reaches Offer Sent → Closed

The daily loop

uae-tick runs the whole thing each Dubai day — replies, due touches, the gated send queue, the weekly scoreboard.

Two hard send gates

Nothing leaves without Finding Verified and Email Verified. Both, every send.

The real bottleneck

Findings, not sends. The inbox ceiling ramps 20 → 25 → 30; that's the number of funnel walks the day actually needs.
