## THE 48-HOUR LEAK FIX — 500 AED, paid after (the entry rung)

The turn-two offer, made the moment a lead engages. Not a track, a rung:
its job is to convert a stranger into a customer at the lowest possible
risk, so that every later number is quoted to someone who has already paid
once.

- **500 AED, paid after.** Access on their side, the one specific banked
  finding fixed and live within 48 hours, paid only once it's live and
  working.
- **Alternative: 365 AED paid up front**, which also includes the full
  12-point teardown of the rest of the funnel. Offered only if they
  hesitate on the shape, never as the opening number.
- **Not gated by `crm-gate offer`.** It is the rung that EARNS the right to
  name the Sprint number, so gating it would deadlock the motion. Only the
  priced Track A / Track B money email needs a gate PASS.
- Statuses: `Leak Fix Sold` → `Leak Fix Delivered`, with the amount in
  `Cash Collected` and `Est. Value` = `Leak Fix (500 AED)`.

Why it replaced the Loom: on the Value Equation a Loom is high effort for
the prospect (watch a video, then decide) and low dream outcome (someone
describing a problem they now feel worse about). It was offered to Ben
Pringle, Lisa Hugo and Lucia Csobonyei and taken by none of them. The paid
fix is the same finding, but the offer is their problem going away, at
zero risk, for the price of a dinner.

## The three laws

**1. Make it instant and effortless, not just impressive.**
Value = (dream outcome x likelihood) / (time delay x effort). Big claims are the crowded half. The winnable half for a solo operator is the bottom of that fraction. An agency quotes 3 weeks and a discovery call. I deliver in 48 hours with one login and zero meetings. That is the moat, so the offer is built on it.

**2. Risk is the whole objection.**
Every question a cold lead asks ("what does it cover", "where are you from", "show me your work") is one question wearing three hats: *is this real*. So the offer leads with risk reversal, not with deliverables. State the worst case unprompted, boldly, before they object.

**3. The price never moves.**
A $700 → $400 → $100 collapse teaches the lead the price was fiction. Three legal moves when they stall, none of them a discount:
- Stall on value → add a **bonus** matched to the stated obstacle
- Objection to a **term** → restructure the term (deposit, timing, payment)
- Objection to **scope** → unbundle

## TRACK A — The 48-Hour Rescue

For the coach already in a warm thread. Smaller audience, low-ticket offers, price-sensitive, risk-scared. The default for this pipeline.

**One-liner:** Everything I found on your site, fixed and live within 48 hours. You click through it all yourself, and you only pay when it works.

**The stack:**
1. Every broken or dead thing fixed (named in HER words, from the email or the video)
2. One place for people to leave their email so the ones finding her stop vanishing
3. BONUS, named and priced: **The Next Three** — a short recorded walkthrough of the three biggest opportunities I found beyond the fixes ($150 value, free with the project). Held-back findings go here, not into free bumps.
4. BONUS: a one-page plain-language note of what changed and why, so she understands her own setup after I'm gone

**Price: $200 flat. In UAE terms, 735 AED.** The number never moves.

**Payment (the risk reversal IS the offer):**
Default ask is half to book the slot. If she hesitates on paying upfront AT ALL, go to full pay-after: "You click through everything, it all works, then you pay." The scope is a day of work, so unpaid delivery is low-risk. The upfront incentive is scheduling, not price: deposit-paid jobs take the current slot, pay-after jobs queue behind them.

**Guarantee, worn boldly, with the or-what:**
> If anything I touched is not working when you check it, you pay nothing and you keep every fix.

Say this in the offer email, not just under pressure.

**Scarcity, honest:** I take one of these at a time so it's genuinely done in 48 hours. One slot this week. True because solo. Sell out and say so.

**Why saying no feels stupid:** costs her one login and zero risk, dead parts of her business work again by the weekend, and if it's not right she keeps the work free. The only thing a no protects is the leak.

## TRACK B — "The Booked-Out Funnel — 5-Day Sprint for UAE Coaches"

For operators who already sell real volume. Anyone with real launch or sales numbers.

Name alternates to test: "The 5-Day Leak-to-Launch", "The Dubai Coach Funnel Sprint". **Always quote it by name and as a stack.** "I'll fix your funnel for 2,575 AED" is a commodity and is directly price-comparable to a Fiverr gig and an Upwork freelancer. A stack whose summed value dwarfs the price is comparable to nothing.

**Price: $700. In UAE terms, 2,575 AED. Open there. Hold it.** The stack and the guarantees are what earn it.

**The stack (register these components and their values verbatim):**

| Component | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Rebuilt opt-in + sales page (copy, design, build) | 4,500 AED |

(The Loom survives here, as a handover deliverable inside a paid project. What died on 2026-07-24 is the free Loom as a turn-two *offer* to an unpaid lead.)

**The two guarantees, stacked, stated boldly and unprompted, by name:**

1. **The Live-or-Free Guarantee** (service):
   > Your funnel is live, tested end-to-end from a clean device, and taking bookings within 5 working days of getting access. If it isn't, you don't pay, and you keep everything I've built.

2. **The First Booking Guarantee** (conditional):
   > If you don't take one booking through the new funnel within 30 days of launch, I keep working, copy, offer, sequence, free, until you do. **Condition: you send traffic to it.**

The condition on ② is load-bearing and is never dropped to sound generous: it aligns incentives and screens out buyers who won't do the work. Risk is the #1 objection and reversing it is worth 2-4x on conversion; before this, there was no guarantee anywhere in the Track B offer at all.

The older order-bump guarantee (*"the bump pays for the project within 30 days of your next launch or I refund the difference"* — receipt: one bump did $522 on a single launch, 1 in 2 buyers taking it) still applies where an order bump is actually in scope. It is a third, situational guarantee, not a replacement for the two named above.

**Bonuses instead of discounts (each named, priced, matched to an objection):**
- **The Leak Map** (900 AED) — kills "what if you miss something"
- **The Send-Ready Launch Kit** (5 emails + 10 captions to point traffic at the new funnel, 1,500 AED) — kills "then what, it just sits there"
- **The 30-Day Tune-Up** (one revision round after real data, 1,200 AED) — kills "what if it doesn't work the first time"
- Situational, where a launch exists: **The Launch-Week Hotline** ($300 value), **The Next Launch Playbook** ($150 value), **The Checkout Second Look** ($200 value)

When a Track B lead stalls, present the bonus that matches the stated obstacle, then ask again. The 2,575 AED never moves. If they push on price rather than value, work the downsell ladder below — never a lower number for the same scope.

**Scarcity and urgency, both honest and true (never invent either):**
- Growth-rate cap: "I take 2 new builds a week. Solo, no team, that's the real ceiling."
- Rolling slot urgency: "next open build slot is [date]."
- Real event deadline when a launch exists (her date is the deadline).

## THE DOWNSELL LADDER — never drop the price for the same thing

"The price never drops, the scope does" is law, but without a named ladder it resolves in the moment, which is exactly when people cave. Standing rungs, worked in order:

1. **Payment Plan Downsell** — 1,300 AED to start, 1,275 AED on launch day. Same total, same scope. First rung on any "it costs too much", because that objection almost always means "it costs too much up front."
2. **Feature Downsell, "The Minimum"** — one flagship page done right (copy, design, build, mobile), 1,800 AED. The name does work: "The Minimum" implies they should get at least that. (This is Avneet's Tier 1, formalised as the standing downsell for everyone.)
3. **The 1-10 check, only after two downsells** — "how badly do you want this fixed, 1 to 10?" 8 or above goes on the payment plan. 7 or below means recombine to whatever their 10 actually is, or let it go.

Never skip to rung 3, never stack two rungs in one email, never invent a rung. A rung is a different deal, not a cheaper one.

## Field rules (so this survives contact with a live thread)

1. **The price never drops.** Bonuses answer stalls. Term restructures answer term objections. Unbundling answers scope objections. The named downsell ladder answers "too expensive". Four different moves, none of them a discount.

2. **Risk reversal goes first, boldly, before she objects — and on Track B that means BOTH guarantees, by name.** The Live-or-Free Guarantee and the First Booking Guarantee (with its traffic condition), stacked, in every money email. "State a guarantee" is not enough instruction; say which. Naming the worst case unprompted ("the most this can cost you is nothing, you keep the work") is the strongest line in the arsenal.

3. **Proof rides with every offer.** She must be able to verify in 30 seconds that I am real, because that is the actual question she is asking.

4. **Deposit default stands until SHE objects.** Pay-after is a designed fallback, not an opener.

5. **Numbers stay straight. Blur them once and the whole proof layer dies:**
   - **$8,123** and **+93%** belong to **Birds & Bees** (single 7-day launch, Dec 2025, one product, no email campaign, her best ever)
   - **$522** and the **50% take rate** belong to **Screen Smart order bumps**
   - **6.6% conversion** belongs to the **Hijab Workbook** (~3x the ~2% industry average)
   - 4x 5-star Upwork reviews

   All from Peace Within Home, which came **INBOUND**. It proves delivery quality and retainer growth. It does NOT prove a cold-outreach method. Never present it as cold-outreach proof.

## UAE-specific note

Track A quoted as **735 AED** (Track B as **2,575 AED**) reads native and avoids the "foreign freelancer converting currencies at me" friction. The dollar number is the same price. Do not treat the AED figure as a discount or a different price, and never quote both currencies at a lead in the same breath.

**The lead must have EARNED a number before this offer goes out.** `python main.py crm-gate offer <row.json>` must print PASS: an earned `Status` (`Call Booked`, `Leak Fix Sold`, `Leak Fix Delivered`, `Offer Sent`, `Won`) or the `Asked For Price` checkbox. See the CRM spec.

The old rule was "price discovery happens BEFORE this offer goes out" — a verbatim answer and an anchor, or no money email. **That was falsified on 2026-07-24**: across 100 touched leads the question was asked 3 times and produced 3 answers, all `Refused to name`, and 0 numbers. Two of the three refusers responded by asking us for a price instead. Nobody names a budget to a stranger over email, and the refusal turned out to be a trust signal, not a price signal — so the answer to it is more risk reversal, never a smaller number. The question moved to the call; `Price Discovery Answer` and `Discovery Anchor` stay in the CRM as advisory data. Their anchor still does not change the price. It is market data.
