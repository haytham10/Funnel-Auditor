# The Outreach Method

Distilled from The Bible (the parenting-pipeline playbook). Everything IG-specific and parenting-specific has been stripped out, because both are dead: the IG account was permanently banned Jun 22 2026, and the niche has moved.

What remains is the part that is actually **proven** and **transfers**.

> Adopted into the repo 2026-07-13 with one correction confirmed by Haytham:
> the original silence-handling section recommended a line opening with "No

> disambiguating question survives; the soft exit does not.

---

## WHAT IS PROVEN (lean on this)

**The opener mechanic works.** A specific verifiable finding + one line of warmth + a real question = a healthy reply rate. Across 125 cold-touched leads and ~364 touches, ~9% of unique leads replied. Industry cold-email benchmark is 1-5%. The mechanic is not the problem.

**The finding is what earns the reply.** Not the pitch, not the credentials, not the offer. The thing they can go check in 30 seconds.

**The soft exit kills warm leads.** "No pressure / no rush / whenever timing's right" appeared at nearly every death point in the old pipeline. This is the most reliable negative finding in the whole dataset.

**A concrete artifact at turn-two is what does NOT kill the lead.** The one lead who ever booked a call got a Loom offer instead of a soft exit at turn-two.

**Single-channel dependency is fatal.** IG suspended once, then permanently banned. Never rely on one platform again.

## WHAT IS UNPROVEN (bets, hold loosely)

**Whether any cold lead closes into money.** ZERO cold closes, ever. 11 replies, 0 closes. Everything above can be true and there is still no business if the close does not happen.

**The price points.** $200 / $700. Reasonable, never validated cold.

**Why leads go quiet after a priced offer.** Only 2 of 11 replies ever surfaced a real priced objection. The rest ghosted, went "not interested," or were fit mismatches. **This is the entire reason the UAE track exists.**

## THE STANDARD MOTION

1. **Find** a coach who passes Gate 0 and Gate 1.
2. **Walk the funnel.** Verify a real finding from a clean state.
3. **Open by email.** Subject + one line of warmth + the specific finding + one real question.
4. **They reply.**
5. **TURN TWO.** One concrete artifact offer OR one single-tap next step. Never a soft exit, never a menu, never a re-pitch.
6. **PRICE DISCOVERY.** *(New in the UAE track. The missing step.)* Before any priced offer: ask what they would pay or what is stopping them.
7. **The priced offer.** GSO v2. The price never moves.
8. **Close.**

## THE FUNNEL WALK (5 stops)

Mechanical, not intuitive. Run on every Gate 1 survivor.

**THE MASTER PRINCIPLE:** a leak is any point where attention the coach already earned fails to convert toward money she can see, AND she would feel the cost if you named it. If something fits this and is not listed below, it is still a leak. If it does not fit, it is not a leak, however "improvable" it looks.

**STOP 1 — The entry point (bio link, homepage, profile link).**
Q: Tap it. Is there ONE clear next step, or a pile of choices and a dead end?
*Principle: the first click should narrow toward a single action.*
Look for: 404s, link trees with 4-5 near-equal links and no "start here", drops onto a bare checkout with no sales page, homepage that leads with a contact form instead of the offer. Catch-all: anything that makes the first click HARDER instead of narrowing it.

**STOP 2 — The free thing.**
Q: Can a stranger get something free right now, and does it ask for an email?
*Principle: a free taste should be reachable AND capture a contact.*
Look for: no free thing at all, buried free thing, only sample locked behind paid signup, delivers with NO email capture. Catch-all: any free-to-contact step that is missing, hidden, or one-directional.

**STOP 3 — The offer page.**
Q: In 10 seconds, can I tell what the paid thing is, the price, and why I would want it?
*Principle: confusion at the buy moment is the most expensive leak. It kills people who were ready.*
Look for: price shown contradictory ways, price hidden entirely, 4-5 near-equal offers with no flagship, wall of text with no hierarchy. Catch-all: anything that makes "what do I buy and why" take longer than ~10 seconds.

**STOP 4 — The checkout / booking.**
Q: If I tried to pay or book right now, would anything stop me?
*Principle: the path from "I want it" to "I paid" must be unbroken.*
Look for: checkout errors, forms that do not submit, login walls, forced account creation, broken payment options, redirect loops, booking calendars with zero available slots. **Bare checkout with no order bump** is a real finding: money not collected at the highest-intent moment. Receipt: $522 from one bump.

**STOP 5 — Do they OWN the audience?**
Q: If the platform vanished tomorrow, could they still reach these people?
*Principle: attention reachable only through a platform they do not control is rented.*
Frame as felt loss, NEVER as "you need a list." Look for: no capture anywhere, traffic sent to affiliate links where the audience's money goes elsewhere.

## THE TWO FILTERS (run on everything the walk flags)

1. **Sting test.** Would she FEEL this, or shrug? If she would shrug ("works fine for me"), it is not an opener even if technically suboptimal.

2. **Vitamin filter.** Is this a FELT COST (money or attention leaking right now) or a MECHANISM she lacks ("you need an email list")? Mechanisms are vitamins. Low cold conversion. Reframe as felt loss, or hold.

A finding that fails either filter is NOT a leak opener.

---

## THE THREE LANES

**LANE 1 — OPEN (felt leak + committed buyer).** The conversion lane. A flagged finding survives both filters. Opener: warmth + the single strongest verified finding + one real question. Favor-framed, no pitch. This lane produces the first close. Always work a real finding when you find one.

**LANE 2 — WARM-UP (committed buyer, no felt leak).** Most profiles land here and that is NORMAL. A felt leak on a real buyer is rare, roughly 20-25% of profiles that pass Gate 1. Opener: not a finding. Enter as a genuine peer, anchored to something specific and real they are doing right now. Goal: intel first, relationship second, conversion third.

**LANE 3 — SKIP (not a buyer).** Hobbyist floor, no income signal, MLM. A felt leak on a non-buyer is still a skip. Leak does not equal buyer.

**Drift-guard:** the warm-up lane is easier, so it tends to crowd out leak-hunting. Lane 1 leads are higher-value. Run the finding scan honestly before defaulting to warm-up.

## VERIFICATION RULES (these saved openers repeatedly)

**Verify from a CLEAN state before opening on it.** A "broken" thing might only be broken for you (login, permission, cache). Check incognito, logged out, first.
- Hard 404 ("file does not exist") = genuinely dead. Openable.
- Permission wall ("you need access") = works fine for her audience. NOT openable.

**"Nothing happened" does not equal broken.** Before opening on "your free thing did not arrive," check whether delivery is AUTOMATED or MANUAL. A solo coach often sends things by hand. Tested once, that looks identical to a broken automation. Look at how she responds to other people. Instant auto-delivery = automated (a real miss is openable). Hand-typed personal replies = manual (your "nothing" just means not-yet, NOT openable).

**Best fit, no clean opener → warm up, do not force it.** Some leads are the strongest fit AND have no verifiable finding. That is a real category, not a failure to look harder. Forcing a manufactured finding burns your best lead. Recognizing "no opener exists here" and NOT sending is a skill, not a loss.

## THE OPENER

- Lead with the specific, verifiable finding. NOT praise. Praise-only openers stall.
- One line of warmth max, then the observation.
- The first line has to earn the second. If it reads like a paste, it is dead.
- End on ONE real question or ONE concrete offer. Not two. Not a CTA plus a question.
- 4-6 lines max. Not a paragraph wall.
- No formal greeting. "Hi [Name], I hope this finds you well" goes in the bin.
- No attachments and no video link on a cold open. Earn the reply first.

**Subject line:**
- Specific, not clever. "your checkout page" beats "quick question" beats "I noticed something."
- Under 8 words.
- Reference something real and specific to them, so it cannot read as a template.
- Never: "I can help you", "free audit", "collaboration", "working with coaches like you."

---

## TURN TWO (the message right after they reply)

This was diagnosed as the leak in the old pipeline. It must contain **ONE concrete artifact offer** (a recorded walkthrough of their live page) **OR one single-tap next step**.

Never:
- A soft exit
- A menu ("call or video?")
- A content-free check-in ("just checking in, how's your week?")
- A re-pitch of the opener

**Kill the soft exit everywhere.** "No pressure / no rush / if it's useful / whenever timing's right" is a reflex, not a choice. Hunt it in every message before sending.

**The nuance:** soft language is fine IF it sits next to a concrete yes. "Want me to mock it up? Free, you can ignore me after" works — the soft part makes the NO painless without removing the YES. "No worries if now's not the time" fails — it removes the yes entirely.

**The test:** is there still a specific, easy thing to say yes to?

## PRICE DISCOVERY (the new step, and the whole point)

Goes out AFTER they reply (usually riding the warmth the artifact created), and always BEFORE any priced offer. Never as a response to a stall — by then the priced offer already went out unanchored and the data point is lost.

One real question, answerable in one line, producing either a number or an obstacle. The canonical phrasings live in the email-draft skill (`references/uae-track.md`). Their answer gets logged VERBATIM in `Price Discovery Answer` and `Discovery Anchor` gets set. The transition to `Offer Sent` is blocked in code until both exist (`python main.py crm-gate offer`).

**Their anchor never changes the price.** A low anchor is market data. It may change which track gets offered, never the number on it.

## SILENCE HANDLING (the thing that actually broke)

The old pipeline's real failure: after a priced offer went quiet, the same offer got re-sent 1-2 more times before anyone asked the disambiguating question.

**When a thread goes quiet after an offer, do not re-send the offer.** Ask the question that actually resolves the ambiguity:
- "Are you already handling this, or would it be easier if I took it off your plate?"
- "Should I stop following up, or is this still on your radar?"

Direct binary questions, no soft exit attached. That gets a real answer or a real close. Another polished bump gets another silent week.

**On email, the persistence lives in the warm sequence and the revival, not in extra cold touches.** The UAE cold sequence is 3 touches (day 0, 3, 9), then Dormant with a revival bump 2-3 weeks out; warm threads get 8-10 touches over weeks. The old pipeline's data (most replies at touches 4-8 over 3-6 weeks) is warm-thread data — the lesson it carries is "never mark a lead Lost early," not "send a fourth cold bump." A cold no-reply is never Lost; it parks as Dormant and comes back with a new angle.

## THE COLD SEQUENCE (three touches, each earns its place)

Day 0, day 3, day 9, then Dormant. Same subject, same thread. There is no touch 4 — a fourth bare bump into silence is a spam signal, not persistence.

Touches 2 and 3 must each carry exactly one NEW thing (enforced at the gate, `crm-gate send --carries …`):

- **The next banked finding** — the walk banks every finding that survives both filters, ranked; touch 1 takes #1, later touches draw the next UNUSED entry. Named as a felt cost with its innocent explanation, fix left vague — naming a second cost is not the Adrienne mistake, teaching a second fix is.
- **The Loom offer** — "want me to record a quick walkthrough showing what I'd fix? easier to show than explain." An offer, never a link, on a cold thread.
- **The disambiguating question** — the direct binary ("Should I stop following up, or is this still on your radar?"). The natural touch 3 closer.

A follow-up that carries none of these is a wasted send eating the day's budget. It doesn't go out.

## THE ARTIFACT (what does the converting)

**A 3-5 minute screen recording of THEIR live page.** Face and voice on. This is the workhorse.

**It contains NOTHING you built.** It is you plus their REAL live page, narrating the finding and describing the fix out loud. No slides, no mockup, no Figma. The entire power is "I looked at YOURS." The moment you swap in anything generic it becomes "here's a thing I made" (a presentation) instead of "here's what's happening to you right now" (a favor).

**Structure:**
- 0:00-0:30 — confirm the finding on their live page
- 0:30-2:30 — show the simple fix
- 2:30-3:00 — what it looks like done
- Then the ask. ONE concrete next step. Do not deflate.

**Deliver FAST.** If you offer it and they say yes, it must exist within the hour (warm) or the day (max). A "yes" that waits 3 days cools to nothing. Do not offer what you cannot produce fast. Be pre-loaded so a yes becomes a delivery in 30 minutes.

**Treat a yes as the STARTING gun, not the finish line.** This is the number one controllable failure point. A yes FEELS like the win, so you relax, and it ships two days late to someone who has cooled.

The artifact is logged in the lead's page body (Email Thread Log, `Artifact:` line) — it has no Status of its own.

## WHEN THEY SAY YES

**"Yeah, send it."** Deliver fast. See above.

**The artifact lands well, they're impressed.** The hinge: free value → paid conversation. The soft-exit habit will betray you here ("glad it helped, no pressure, lmk!" = thread dies warm). The move is ONE concrete next step — and in this track, that next step is the price discovery question, then the offer.

**They want to talk money.** Traps: under-charging from nerves, over-scoping to justify a price, freezing with no quote ready. Decide the offer and price BEFORE the conversation. It is already decided: GSO v2. The price never moves. If they jump straight to "how much," get the discovery answer first: give the flip question (see the email-draft skill), log their number, then quote.

**Emotional rule:** do not lunge past the value to the close (eager). Do not under-ask and give it away free (nervous). Calm, deliver fast, one clear ask per step.

---

## THE NUMBERS RULE

**Always compute rates by LEAD, never by message.** Counting message-rows instead of unique leads inflates the reply rate and produces a false picture. This happened once and it mattered.

**Update the scoreboard weekly.** The old pipeline's numbers sat frozen for two weeks while real sends kept happening, which is a big part of why it felt broken when it was actually outperforming benchmark. Sends, replies, discovery answers, closes. Two minutes. (The `uae-tick` skill runs this automatically once a week.)
